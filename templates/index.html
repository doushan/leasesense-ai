About This System

MSc AIML Dissertation — University of Technology, Mauritius

🎯
Research Objective
This MVP demonstrates the feasibility of AI-driven lease lifecycle management for vehicle leasing SMEs in Mauritius. It addresses Research Objective O2: developing a minimum viable AI framework combining workflow automation and predictive analytics.
📊
Dataset
Models are trained on a stratified 1,000-record sample from the L&T Vehicle Loan Default Prediction dataset (233,154 records, Kaggle). This simulates the data volume available to a typical Mauritian leasing SME. The 78:22 non-default to default ratio is preserved.
⚙️
Methodology
Three classical ML models are compared: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest. Preprocessing includes outlier capping, median imputation, SMOTE for class balancing, and Min-Max scaling. Hyperparameters are tuned via stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
🔬
6 Engineered Features
LTV Ratio · Credit History Flag · Account Activity Ratio · Primary Delinquency Rate · Age at Disbursement · Employment Tenure Proxy. These domain-specific features were designed to be interpretable by non-technical leasing staff.
📍
Context
Mauritius has over 600,000 registered vehicles and a growing leasing industry dominated by SMEs managing 50–500 live leases. Most still rely on manual, paper-based processes. This system addresses that gap with a cost-effective, interpretable AI solution.
